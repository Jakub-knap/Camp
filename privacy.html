2. What data we process and why

  • Account: email, name and profile photo from your Google account, account identifier, date of first sign-in and of accepting the terms. Purpose: sign-in and running the service. Legal basis: performance of a contract (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR).
  • Content you add: crews and their names, personal and shared packing lists (names, photos, categories, who added and packed an item), custom categories, chat messages including photos and reactions, and information on who read the messages and when. Legal basis: performance of a contract.
  • Invitations: the email address of the person you invite to a crew and who invited them. We send the invitee a notification in the app and one invitation email. Legal basis: legitimate interest in letting you invite your friends (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR). If someone does not accept the invitation, their address is removed once the invitation is declined or cancelled.
  • Notifications: if you allow them, we store a technical identifier of your device so we can send you notifications. Legal basis: your consent, which you withdraw by turning notifications off on your device.
  • Payment: when you buy the full version, the payment is processed by Stripe. We receive the email, amount, date and status of the payment — we never see your card details. Legal basis: performance of a contract and legal obligations (accounting, Art. 6(1)(c) GDPR).
  • Technical data: app settings (language, last opened world and crew) and temporary data for offline use are stored on your device. Legal basis: legitimate interest in the app working properly.

3. Who sees your data

  • Only you can see your personal packing list.
  • Shared items, the chat, your name and profile photo are visible to members of the crews you are in.
  • The operator accesses the data only to the extent strictly necessary (e.g. when solving a problem you report).

4. Processors

To run the service we use the following providers, who process data on our behalf:

  • Google (Firebase, Gmail) — sign-in, database (data stored in a data centre in Frankfurt), delivery of notifications, sending invitation emails, website visit statistics.
  • Vercel — website hosting and server functions (sending notifications and invitations).
  • Stripe — payment processing.

Some of them are companies based in the USA. Data transfers take place under the EU–US Data Privacy Framework or standard contractual clauses approved by the European Commission.

5. How long we keep data

  • Account data and your personal list — as long as the account exists.
  • Shared items and messages — until someone deletes them or the organizer deletes the crew. If you leave a crew, your earlier messages remain visible to the other members.
  • Invitations — until accepted, declined or cancelled.
  • Payment data — 10 years, as required by accounting law.

When your account is closed, we delete your data, except data we must keep by law.

6. Cookies and on-device storage

Essential: the app stores data on your device that is needed for sign-in, offline use and remembering settings. The app would not work without it, so no consent is needed.

Statistics (Google Analytics): only on the home page and only with your consent. They help us see how many people visit the site. You can change your consent at any time via the "Cookie settings" link in the footer of the home page. The app itself uses no statistics or advertising cookies.

7. Your rights

You have the right to access your data, to rectification, erasure, restriction of processing and data portability, the right to object to processing based on legitimate interest, and the right to withdraw consent at any time. Send your request (including a request to close your account and delete all data) to campsync1@gmail.com from your account email. We will reply within 30 days at the latest.

8. Complaints

If you believe we are handling your data incorrectly, you can lodge a complaint with the Office for Personal Data Protection of the Slovak Republic, dataprotection.gov.sk.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy. We will notify you of material changes in the app; the current version is always on this page.
