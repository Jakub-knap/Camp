2. The service

CampSync is an app for groups ("crews") heading out together camping, fishing or to a cabin. It lets you create crews, invite people, keep a personal and a shared packing list (what is missing and what is packed), write in a shared chat and receive notifications.

The app is available as a web app (PWA) and as an Android app. Sign-in is via a Google account. The service is intended for people aged 16 and over.
